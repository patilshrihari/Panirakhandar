How water is shared

  • Allocatable water = available water × (1 − reserve %).
  • Farmer allocation = allocatable water × (farmer demand ÷ total demand).
  • A farmer is never given more than their own demand, and the total never exceeds the allocatable water.
  • Demand = the farmer’s total daily need × planning days ().
  • If water is short, every farmer receives the same share of their demand, and the shortage is shown below.
