🌾 Farmers & Water Demand

Register farmers and their farms, and estimate how much irrigation water each farm needs per day and over the summer.

📆 Planning period

Number of summer days you want to irrigate. Seasonal demand = total daily demand × planning days. All volumes are in cubic metres (m³).

🌱 Crop water assumptions (editable)

Optional helper values in m³ per hectare per day. Panirakhandar does not ship real crop requirements: enter figures from your local agriculture office or your own experience. They are only used by the “Estimate from crop assumption” button, and you can always type a different daily need for each farm.
