💧 Smart Pond Planning

Work out how much storage is needed to carry monsoon water into summer, and whether the stream can actually fill it.

Planning estimate, not a construction design. Real ponds need local engineering, safety, environmental and legal assessment (soil, seepage, embankment safety, land rights, permissions and downstream impact).

Pond assumptions

Seepage + evaporation over the storage period. Must be below 100.
Extra water kept on top of irrigation demand for uncertainty or other needs.

How this is calculated

  • Required irrigation storage = seasonal irrigation demand (from the Farmers page).
  • Reserve = demand × reserve %. It is added to the requirement, so the water that must reach the fields is demand × (1 + reserve %).
  • Storage target before losses = (demand + reserve) ÷ (1 − loss %). Losses are counted once, here, so the pond must hold extra to cover them.
  • Usable capacity per pond = nominal capacity × (1 − loss %).
  • Minimum pond count = rounded-up (target ÷ nominal capacity). Same loss % as above, not applied again.
  • Surplus / shortage = capturable monsoon water − storage target. Ponds cannot create water: if the stream cannot supply the target, the shortfall stays a shortfall however many ponds you build.
