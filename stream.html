🌊 Stream Water Assessment

Estimate how much water your village nala carries in the monsoon and in summer, and how much of it could realistically be captured.

Estimates only. Variable flow, irregular stream cross-sections, measurement errors and the need to leave water in the stream for downstream users and wildlife (environmental flow) all affect accuracy. The “capturable” figure is only the share you choose to capture. It is not the whole seasonal flow.
