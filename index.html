Smart Village Water Management

Panirakhandar

Save Water Today. Sustain Farms Tomorrow.

Monsoon streams in the Western Ghats carry plenty of water, yet sugarcane farms run dry in summer. Panirakhandar (pani-water)+ (Rakhandar-Gaurdian)helps a village estimate what its nala(stream) carries, plan storage ponds, and share summer irrigation water fairly.
